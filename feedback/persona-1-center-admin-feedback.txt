 PERSONA FEEDBACK — CENTER ADMIN ("Amira")
 Persona 1 of 4 · ABA Therapy Platform · MVP feedback pass
=====================================================================

WHO I AM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Amira, 41 — founder/director of a small ABA center in Dubai.
4 supervisors, 12 therapists, ~28 active students. Not technical —
she hired you (or a tool) so she doesn't have to think about
servers, plans, or passwords. She cares about: enrollment growing,
parents happy, staff accountable, and money coming in on time.

MY GOALS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Run the center from one place: staff, students, reports, billing.
- Know that parents are seeing their child's progress (retention!).
- Make sure notes are co-signed and data is complete (quality).
- Not lose students because the tool is slow or confusing.

WHAT I LOVED (keep doing this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
+ One place for everything: staff, students, programs, sessions,
  reports, calendar — I don't juggle spreadsheets anymore.
+ The Today/Week/Month toggle with trend arrows — I finally see
  "are we busier than last month?" at a glance.
+ Staff can only see their assigned students. When I heard about
  the privacy fix I felt much safer recommending this to parents.
+ Parents have their own portal and can't touch anything. Fewer
  panicked phone calls asking "did the therapist write notes?"
+ Billing page: I can see my plan, days left in trial, students
  used vs. my limit, and I understand what I'm paying for.

MY FRUSTRATIONS (fix these)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADDING A STUDENT IS A DEAD END WHEN THE LIMIT HITS
   When I hit my plan's student cap, adding a student just fails.
   The upgrade prompt exists, but the flow feels like punishment:
   I'm busy, I need to enroll a child NOW. I want: "You're at 10/10
   on Basic. Upgrade to Pro and continue adding this student" —
   one click, carry over the info I already typed.

2. NO WAY TO RE-ORDER / RE-ASSIGN ON STAFF ABSENCE
   A therapist calls in sick. I want to re-assign today's sessions
   to another therapist in two clicks. Today I go into the student,
   unassign, reassign... it's slow and I'm scared I'll break
   something. A "reassign today's sessions" action would save me.

3. REPORTS ARE NUMBERS, NOT ANSWERS
   Reports show totals + trends, which is nice, but I can't answer
   the questions parents actually ask me: "Is my child improving?"
   "Show me 3 months." I want an exportable, parent-friendly
   progress report per student (PDF) I can print or email.

4. NO ATTENDANCE MODULE YET
   I track attendance on paper. The database has attendance tables
   (I was told), but there's nothing for me to click. Invoicing
   parents per attended session is manual. This is my #1 most
   wanted feature.

5. PASSWORD RESETS ARE ALL ON ME
   Every "I forgot my password" comes to me — I log into the Users
   page, edit the account, set a temp password, phone the staff
   member/parent. It's 5–10 minutes each time, several times a
   week. A "reset password" button that emails a link would give
   me my life back.

6. NO NOTIFICATIONS WHEN STAFF FALL BEHIND
   I find out a note wasn't written when I look at the Pending
   Notes card. I want an alert (or a weekly email digest):
   "3 notes pending > 48h, 2 sessions never ended properly."

7. TRIAL & BILLING ANXIETY
   The trial banner shows days left, but nothing tells me what
   happens ON the expiry day: do I get locked out? Does my data
   survive? I don't want to find out the hard way. A clear
   "your trial ends in 3 days — here's what happens" email/screen.

8. ACCOUNT-LEVEL SETTINGS ARE MISSING
   Center name, logo, working hours, session lengths, currency...
   I expected a Settings page. There isn't one. Also no way to
   see my own subscription invoices without Stripe portal (fine,
   but link it clearly).

9. ONBOARDING A NEW STAFF MEMBER IS CLUNKY
   I create the account with a temp password and have to give it
   verbally. No invite email, no "force password change on first
   login." Feels insecure and unprofessional.

10. MOBILE ISN'T REAL
    I checked the mobile app — it's a login screen and nothing
    else. I expected at least "view today's schedule" on my phone
    between meetings. I use my phone 60% of the time.

TOP 5 FIXES I'D PAY FOR TOMORROW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Attendance + per-session parent invoicing
 2. Parent-friendly student progress report (PDF export)
 3. Password reset (self-serve, emailed link)
 4. Smooth "at limit → upgrade without losing my typed data"
 5. Alerts for pending notes / incomplete sessions (admin digest)

EMOTIONAL ARC (how the app makes me feel)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Excited (all-in-one!) → confident (reports look professional) →
annoyed (limit dead-end, manual attendance) → anxious (trial
expiry, password babysitting) → hopeful (if they fix 1–5, I stay).
