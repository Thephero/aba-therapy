 3) THE OWNER PRICING DASHBOARD (new page)
================================================================
Where:  https://azm-therapy.vercel.app/dashboard/platform
        (also reachable from the sidebar "Platform Pricing" item —
         only the platform owner account sees it)

Who:    owner@aba-demo.com / owner123

What you can edit there:
  - Currency (USD, AED, ...) and base monthly price
  - How many students the base plan includes
  - Turn yearly (annual) billing on with its price
  - Each add-on: label, price, monthly vs one-off, on/off
  - One "Save" button at the bottom.

How saving works:
  - The new prices apply IMMEDIATELY for every center (no
    redeploy, no restart). Limit enforcement and the Billing
    page update instantly.
  - Stripe price objects are created/refreshed automatically
    when Stripe is connected. Price IDs are immutable, so the
    system always switches to the newest price on the next
    checkout — nothing to do manually.

 6) IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================
- STRIPE IS NOT YET CONNECTED. Everything (pricing edits, limits,
  billing page) works without Stripe — the checkout buttons only
  say "payments not configured". To turn real charging on:
     1. Create the Stripe (test) price(s) — the app creates the
        Price objects itself once you save pricing with a key set,
        so in most cases you only need these env vars on Render:
             STRIPE_SECRET_KEY
             STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET
     2. Add the Stripe webhook endpoint:
             https://aba-therapy-backend-7l47.onrender.com/api/v1/webhooks/stripe
        (events: checkout.session.completed, customer.subscription.*,
         invoice.payment_failed)
- FREE-TIER SLEEP: after ~15 min idle, the backend stops; the
  first click afterwards takes 30–60 s to load. That is normal.
- Safe habits if you ever run anything local: never commit
  secrets; production DB scripts ask for a postgresql:// URL.
