=====================================================================
 CUSTOMER JOURNEY MAP — PARENT / GUARDIAN ("Layla")
 Persona 4 of 4 · draw-ready format (one column per stage)
=====================================================================

HOW TO DRAW THIS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Horizontal axis = the 6 STAGES below (left → right).
- Swimlanes per stage: ACTIONS, TOUCHPOINTS, THOUGHTS, EMOTION
  (1–10), PAIN POINTS, OPPORTUNITIES.
- Emotion line chart across stages. 😡=2 … 🤩=9.
- Journey = first 30 days as a portal parent (emotional journey —
  this persona churns the CENTER, not the app).

JOURNEY SCOPE: "From receiving my login to trusting the center
with my child's story."

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1 — INVITATION & FIRST LOGIN           [EMOTION: 5 😐]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Receives URL + temp password (via WhatsApp from admin).
  - Logs in on her phone; no welcome screen, no forced password
    change.
  - Doesn't know the user manual exists.
TOUCHPOINTS: WhatsApp message · login page · dashboard (first view)
THOUGHTS: "Okay... I have an account. Now what am I looking at?"
PAIN POINTS:
  - Cold onboarding: no welcome tour, no plain-language intro,
    password she can't change herself.
  - URL + password over WhatsApp feels insecure.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - 3-step welcome (change password → meet your child's team →
    see this week's plan).
  - Email invite instead of WhatsApp.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2 — EXPLORING MY CHILD'S WORLD         [EMOTION: 7 😊 PEAK]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Opens My Dashboard: child profile card, stats, 30-day chart.
  - Opens My Child: care team faces, programs & skills, history.
  - (Two kids? Tries the child selector — it works, one tap.)
TOUCHPOINTS: /dashboard/parent · /dashboard/parent/student ·
child selector
THOUGHTS: "I can SEE his programs and who works with him. Most
centers never show parents any of this."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Jargon wall: "mand," "DTT," "SOAP," "prompt level" mean
    nothing to her.
  - The chart shows SESSION COUNTS — busy-ness, not progress.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Plain-language mode / tooltips per term.
  - Skill-progress view per program ("Identify Red: 40% → 80%").

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3 — READING A SESSION                  [EMOTION: 4 😕]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Opens Session History → a completed session → sees trial
    counts and behavior numbers in a table.
  - Tries to understand what it means for her child. Fails.
TOUCHPOINTS: parent student page → session history rows
THOUGHTS: "8 trials, 2 behaviors... is that good? What did he
actually DO today? I just want the story of his day."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Data without narrative: no plain-language session summary.
  - Cannot ask anyone about it in-app → WhatsApp/call the center.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Auto "parent summary" per session (plain language, 3 lines).
  - Reply/ask button on each session → goes to supervisor.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4 — THE CALENDAR & DAILY LOGISTICS     [EMOTION: 6 🙂]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Uses parent calendar: color dots (blue upcoming, green done,
    red cancelled), day detail panel, upcoming/past sidebar.
  - Plans pickups and days off around it.
TOUCHPOINTS: /dashboard/parent/calendar
THOUGHTS: "The color calendar is genuinely useful."
PAIN POINTS:
  - NO notifications: a cancellation reaches her only when she
    next opens the portal (or worse, at pickup).
  - Times lack timezone clarity; occasionally "disagrees" with
    her phone clock.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Push/email notifications: reminders, cancellations, new
    notes.
  - Timezone label on all times.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 5 — MONEY & PAPERWORK                  [EMOTION: 3 😣 LOWEST]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Needs the assessment report for SCHOOL — asks the center,
    receives it by WhatsApp/email.
  - Wants to check what she owes → nothing in the portal →
    calls admin → gets a number → pays by transfer.
TOUCHPOINTS: WhatsApp · phone · bank transfer (ALL outside app)
THOUGHTS: "The portal shows progress but not paperwork or
payments. Why is the important stuff still on WhatsApp?"
PAIN POINTS:
  - No Documents area (DocumentUpload model exists, unused).
  - No invoice/financial visibility for parents.
  - Reports/letters live outside the app — no single source of
    truth.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Documents section (reports, letters, invoices) per child.
  - Read-only invoice history + "amount due" card.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 6 — BEING A PARTNER (ONGOING)          [EMOTION: 5 😐]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Checks the portal 2–3× a week; re-reads the chart.
  - Wants to help at home but has no idea what to practice.
  - Asks other parents which centers give "real updates."
TOUCHPOINTS: portal · WhatsApp · parent community (external)
THOUGHTS: "If they sent me home activities and answered my
questions here, this would be my favorite thing about the center."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No home-practice/activity feed.
  - One-way portal: she consumes but never contributes or asks.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - "This week's home practice" posted by the supervisor.
  - Two-way messaging (even with one admin) + satisfaction check
    after each month.

=====================================================================
EMOTION CURVE (for the line chart)
  Stage 1: 5 → Stage 2: 7 (PEAK: visibility builds trust) →
  Stage 3: 4 (jargon + no story) → Stage 4: 6 → Stage 5: 3
  (LOWEST: paperwork/payments outside the app) → Stage 6: 5
BIGGEST LEAK: Stage 5 (documents/invoices) then Stage 3 (session
story). Layla's trust peaks at visibility and crashes where the
app forces her back to WhatsApp. Fix those two and she becomes
the center's best referral channel.
