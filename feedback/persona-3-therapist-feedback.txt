 PERSONA FEEDBACK — THERAPIST / RBT ("Sara")
 Persona 3 of 4 · ABA Therapy Platform · MVP feedback pass
=====================================================================

WHO I AM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Sara, 26 — RBT therapist, 6 students, 5–6 sessions a day, one on
top of the other. I'm on my feet with kids all day. I use the app
between sessions and during them, usually one-handed, often on my
phone (which... the app isn't really made for). Speed = my sanity.

MY GOALS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Record data fast and correctly, without breaking the session flow.
- Never lose a note or forget a session end.
- Know exactly what each child's targets/programs are today.
- Go home on time — paperwork shouldn't eat my evening.

WHAT I LOVED (keep doing this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
+ Pending Notes card tells me what I owe before I go home.
+ "Run a Session" button takes me straight to starting — good.
+ The duration timer for behavior logging is right there with
  Start/Stop/Reset. I don't need a stopwatch and a notepad.
+ Auto-SOAP drafts the note from my data — I fix a few lines
  instead of writing from memory 4 hours later.
+ Editable SOAP until finalized — I can correct a typo after the
  session ends without begging the supervisor.

MY FRUSTRATIONS (fix these)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TOO MANY TAPS FOR EVERY DATA POINT
   Record Trial = open modal → pick program → pick target → pick
   outcome → response → prompt level → save. In a real session,
   with a kid waiting, I sometimes skip trials. I want a
   quick-record mode: tap target chip → correct/incorrect. Two
   taps, then details optional later.

2. IT'S NOT PHONE-FRIENDLY
   The layout assumes a laptop. On my phone: tiny targets,
   dropdowns are painful, modals overflow. And the mobile app is
   an empty shell. I need the session flow on a phone, period.
   This is my #1 ask.

3. I CAN'T SEE THE SESSION PLAN AT A GLANCE
   When I open a student, I want "today's targets," prompt
   hierarchy, and the behavior plan right at the top — a
   therapist view. Right now I scroll through everything the
   supervisor sees and hunt.

4. LOGIN FAILURES ARE A BLACK BOX
   When the server hiccups (it happened twice this month), I see
   "Invalid email or password" — I reset my password thinking it's
   me, then find out the server was down. Tell me the difference.

5. NO OFFLINE / FLAKY-WIFI SAFETY
   Centers have dead zones. If the app loses connection
   mid-session I'm not sure if my trial was saved. I want a clear
   "saved / retry" state — or offline capture that syncs later.

6. CALENDAR DOESN'T HELP MY DAY
   The calendar shows sessions but doesn't answer "what's my
   next session, where, with whom" instantly. A "My Day" list
   (next up, room, notes pending) would beat a grid.

7. MAND/PREFERENCE RECORDING FEELS BOLTED ON
   Mands: open modal, pick type, item, prompt, independence...
   that's 4 fields for what is often "he asked for water
   independently." Quick-tally buttons (+1 spontaneous, +1
   prompted) would be so much faster.

8. NO WAY TO FLAG SOMETHING TO MY SUPERVISOR
   If a kid has a rough session or I notice something, I have no
   in-app way to attach a note/flag for the supervisor. Back to
   WhatsApp. There's a messages table in the backend but no UI.

9. PASSWORD RESET = PHONE TAG
   I text the admin, wait, get a temp password, log in, it's
   still awkward. Self-serve reset please.

10. END-OF-DAY PAPERWORK PILE-UP
    Notes after the last session of the day are the worst. I'd
    love a "wrap-up" checklist: sessions today → notes pending →
    write in 2 minutes with auto-SOAP → done. It's half there
    (auto-SOAP + pending notes) — connect the dots.

TOP 5 FIXES I'D PAY FOR TOMORROW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Mobile-friendly session recording (or a real mobile app)
 2. Quick-trial mode (2-tap data capture)
 3. "My Day" view (next session, room, targets at a glance)
 4. Clear save/sync status + offline tolerance
 5. Clear login errors (server down ≠ wrong password)

EMOTIONAL ARC
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Optimistic (clean screens) → stressed (tap-heavy mid-session) →
resentful (laptop-first in a phone world) →
relieved (auto-SOAP + timer) →
pleading ("just make it fast on my phone and I'll love it").
