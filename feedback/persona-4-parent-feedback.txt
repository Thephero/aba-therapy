=====================================================================
 PERSONA FEEDBACK — PARENT / GUARDIAN ("Layla")
 Persona 4 of 4 · ABA Therapy Platform · MVP feedback pass
=====================================================================

WHO I AM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Layla, 37 — mother of Youssef (6), diagnosed ASD level 1, in
therapy for 14 months. Works part-time. Emotional stake: 100%.
She checks the portal after work and before parent meetings.
She is NOT technical and is easily lost by jargon (DTT, mand,
SOAP mean nothing to her).

MY GOALS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- See that my child is progressing — with my own eyes, in plain
  language.
- Know what happens in sessions and how I can help at home.
- Trust the center: professional communication, no chasing.
- Feel like a partner, not a bystander.

WHAT I LOVED (keep doing this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
+ I have a portal AT ALL. Most centers give me nothing. I can see
  my child's sessions, programs, and a progress chart whenever I
  want — that alone builds trust.
+ The child selector at the top — I have two kids at the center
  and switching between them is one tap.
+ The calendar with color-coded dots (green = done, blue =
  upcoming) — I can plan pickups and time off around it.
+ I can't accidentally change anything. Read-only sounds limiting
  but honestly it makes me feel the data is real.
+ Privacy: I only see MY children. Nothing else leaks in.

MY FRUSTRATIONS (fix these)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE LANGUAGE IS CLINICAL, I'M NOT
   "Mand," "DTT," "SOAP note," "prompt level" — I don't know what
   these mean. I want plain-language labels with a tiny "what is
   this?" tooltip, or a parent-mode toggle that translates.

2. THE CHART SHOWS COUNTS, NOT PROGRESS
   "Sessions completed over 30 days" tells me how BUSY the center
   is, not whether my child is LEARNING. I want: skill progress
   per program (e.g., "Identify Red: 40% → 80% mastered"),
   in simple up/down language with dates.

3. SESSION HISTORY IS DATA, NOT A STORY
   Rows with trial counts are for clinicians. I want a short
   plain-language summary per session: "Today we worked on
   colors — Youssef got 8/10 right, needed less help than last
   week." Even an auto-generated "parent summary" would be gold.

4. I CAN'T MESSAGE ANYONE
   When I have a question ("can we move Thursday?") I call or
   WhatsApp. If a message tab existed — even to one admin — I'd
   use it constantly. Backend has messages; portal doesn't.

5. NO DOCUMENTS / REPORTS SECTION
   Assessment reports, invoices, medical letters I need for
   school — all sent by WhatsApp/email outside the app. The
   DocumentUpload table exists but there's nothing for me.
   A "Documents" area would make this my single source of truth.

6. NO HOME ACTIVITIES
   The #1 parent ask in ABA: "what can I do at home?" Even a
   simple "this week's home practice" posted by the supervisor
   would make me feel like a partner, not a spectator.

7. NO NOTIFICATIONS AT ALL
   If a session is cancelled while I'm at work, I find out when
   I arrive. I need optional push/email: session reminders,
   cancellations, "new note posted."

8. BILLING/INVOICES ARE INVISIBLE TO ME
   I pay per session (or a package). Nothing in the portal shows
   what I owe or what's paid. Even read-only invoice history
   would stop the monthly "how much do I owe?" call.

9. FIRST LOGIN EXPERIENCE IS COLD
   I received an email-ish text with a temp password and a URL.
   No welcome, no "here's what you'll see," no forced password
   change. A 3-step welcome would make a big difference in trust.

10. TIMEZONE/DATE CONFUSION (small but real)
    Session times show without timezone context; my phone clock
    and the calendar occasionally disagree. Label times with the
    center's timezone.

TOP 5 FIXES I'D PAY FOR TOMORROW (or stay longer because of)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Plain-language parent summaries per session
 2. Skill-progress view per program (not just session counts)
 3. Messaging with the center
 4. Notifications (cancellations + reminders)
 5. Documents area (reports + invoices)

EMOTIONAL ARC
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Grateful (a portal exists!) → curious (explore everything) →
confused (clinical jargon) → frustrated (chart ≠ progress) →
disconnected (no way to ask/talk) →
hopeful (plain language + messaging + documents = I'd recommend
this center to every parent I know).
