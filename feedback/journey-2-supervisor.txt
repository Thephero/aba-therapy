=====================================================================
 CUSTOMER JOURNEY MAP — SUPERVISOR / BCBA ("Omar")
 Persona 2 of 4 · draw-ready format (one column per stage)
=====================================================================

HOW TO DRAW THIS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Horizontal axis = the 6 STAGES below (left → right).
- Swimlanes per stage: ACTIONS, TOUCHPOINTS, THOUGHTS, EMOTION
  (1–10), PAIN POINTS, OPPORTUNITIES.
- Emotion line chart across stages. 😡=2 … 🤩=9.
- This is a WEEKLY LOOP journey — stages 4–6 repeat every week.

JOURNEY SCOPE: "From new student intake to co-signed note" —
the clinical quality cycle Omar lives in.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1 — INTAKE & ASSESSMENT                [EMOTION: 8 😃]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Admin adds new student, assigns him as supervisor.
  - Runs VB-MAPP or ABLLS-R: setup → score entry → submit.
  - Reviews evaluation dashboard: totals, deficits, flags,
    suggested goals.
TOUCHPOINTS: student profile · Assessments (grids, master grid,
deficit list, suggested goals)
THOUGHTS: "The grid tool saves me hours. This is genuinely good."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Accepted suggested goals do NOT flow into Programs — he must
    re-create every target by hand (double entry).
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - One-click "accept goal → creates target" bridge.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2 — PROGRAM DESIGN                     [EMOTION: 7 🙂]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Creates programs (e.g., Color Identification) per student.
  - Adds targets: domain, prompt hierarchy, mastery criteria.
  - Creates behavior plans (FREQUENCY vs DURATION) with ABC
    options locked for therapists.
TOUCHPOINTS: Programs page (Manage modal) · student profile
(behavior plans card)
THOUGHTS: "Locked data types = consistent data. Love it."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No program templates ("copy program from similar student").
  - Prompt-performance data isn't charted per target yet.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Program templates library; learning-curve charts per target.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3 — ASSIGN & SCHEDULE                  [EMOTION: 5 😐]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Assigns therapists to the student.
  - Schedules sessions for the week (one by one).
TOUCHPOINTS: student detail (assign) · Sessions page (Schedule
button) · Calendar
THOUGHTS: "Every child has a fixed weekly schedule — why am I
entering the same slot 12 times?"
PAIN POINTS:
  - No recurring sessions.
  - No therapist-availability view; conflicts discovered late.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - "Every Sun/Tue 4pm × 12 weeks" recurrence.
  - Availability/conflict indicators in calendar.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4 — DAILY DATA REVIEW (WEEKLY LOOP)    [EMOTION: 6 🙂]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Opens each of ~10 students to check trial accuracy, behavior
    counts, mand data from yesterday's sessions.
  - Looks for stalling targets by scrolling tables manually.
TOUCHPOINTS: student profiles · session details · Programs page
THOUGHTS: "I need ONE screen: which kids are stalling this week?"
PAIN POINTS:
  - NO risk overview: stalling/regression must be hunted student
    by student. (Assessment flags exist but only inside
    assessments.)
  - No inter-observer/implausible-data checks.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Supervisor home: risk-sorted list (no progress in N days,
    accuracy drop >20%, notes unsigned).
  - Data-quality flags (e.g., 100% on 40 trials in 10 min).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 5 — COACHING & INSTRUCTIONS            [EMOTION: 4 😟]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Adjusts a target mid-week; needs to tell the therapist NOW.
  - Falls back to WhatsApp because there is no in-app messaging.
  - Reviews behavior sessions via screen-share or in person.
TOUCHPOINTS: WhatsApp (external!) · phone calls · in-person
THOUGHTS: "Clinical instructions live in WhatsApp. If a therapist
loses their phone, my clinical decisions are gone."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Message/Attendance/Document models exist in the DB but have
    NO UI — the single biggest gap for his role.
  - No way to attach a comment to a specific target or session.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - In-app messaging per student (therapist ↔ supervisor).
  - Comments/notes pinned to targets and sessions.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 6 — NOTE REVIEW & CO-SIGN (WEEKLY)     [EMOTION: 5 😐]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Finds notes to sign one by one: open session → read SOAP →
    check data → Co-sign & Finalize → find next session manually.
  - Edits therapist notes when drafts are weak (auto-SOAP helps).
TOUCHPOINTS: session detail page (SOAP card, Co-sign & Finalize)
THOUGHTS: "Signing is fine; FINDING what needs signing is the job."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No co-sign queue; no "notes awaiting me" counter on his
    dashboard (Pending Notes shows the THERAPIST's debt, not his).
  - Finalized notes are locked — a late correction requires
    workarounds.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Co-sign queue with data beside the note + bulk sign.
  - "Notes awaiting YOUR signature" dashboard card.

=====================================================================
EMOTION CURVE (for the line chart)
  Stage 1: 8 (peak: assessments) → Stage 2: 7 → Stage 3: 5 →
  Stage 4: 6 → Stage 5: 4 (LOWEST: WhatsApp dependency) →

BIGGEST LEAK: Stage 5 (messaging) and Stage 4 (no risk view).
