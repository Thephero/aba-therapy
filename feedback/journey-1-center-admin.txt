STAGE 1 — DISCOVERY / SIGN-UP                [EMOTION: 7 😊 hopeful]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Hears about platform, opens website.
  - Registers center → 14-day trial starts automatically.
  - Reads user manual linked from login page.
TOUCHPOINTS: marketing/website · registration form · trial banner
THOUGHTS: "Finally, something built for ABA centers."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No guided setup wizard: after sign-up she stares at an empty
    dashboard ("now what?").
  - Nothing tells her what happens at trial end (fear of lockout).
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - 5-step onboarding checklist (add staff → add students →
    create programs → schedule → invite parents).
  - "What happens after the trial?" explainer on the billing page.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2 — SETUP (STAFF + STUDENTS)           [EMOTION: 6 🙂 willing]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Creates staff accounts (supervisors, therapists) with temp
    passwords.
  - Adds students; assigns supervisor + therapists per student.
  - Links parent accounts to children.
TOUCHPOINTS: Users page · Students page · student detail page
THOUGHTS: "This is all manual but at least it's all in one place."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No invite emails — she hands out passwords by phone/WhatsApp.
  - No "force password change on first login" — unprofessional.
  - Bulk import (Excel/CSV) of students missing — typing 28
    students one by one took a whole evening.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Email invitations with self-set passwords.
  - CSV import for students; bulk staff invite.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3 — FIRST WEEK OF OPERATIONS           [EMOTION: 7 😊]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Supervisors create programs/targets/behavior plans.
  - Therapists run sessions; she watches the dashboard.
  - Checks Quick Stats + trends daily (Today/Week/Month toggle).
TOUCHPOINTS: Dashboard · MiniDashboard · Sessions page
THOUGHTS: "I can finally see if we're busy. This looks professional
to show investors/partners."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Reports show counts, not clinical outcomes — she can't answer
    a parent's "is my child improving?" from the Reports page.
  - Staff absences = manual reassignment shuffle (slow, scary).
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Per-student progress report (PDF, parent-friendly).
  - "Reassign today's sessions" quick action.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4 — BILLING DECISION (END OF TRIAL)    [EMOTION: 4 😟 anxious]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Receives no reminder; opens billing page to check days left.
  - Compares tiers (Basic 199 / Pro 449 / Ent 899 AED; annual ×10).
  - Clicks Subscribe → Stripe Checkout → card → back to app.
TOUCHPOINTS: Billing page · Stripe Checkout · Stripe Customer Portal
THOUGHTS: "The prices are clear... but what EXACTLY happens if the
trial expires before I decide? Will I lose access to my data?"
PAIN POINTS:
  - No email reminder before trial end.
  - Dead-end when student limit reached mid-enrollment (typed data
    at risk of loss; must upgrade before finishing).
  - Stripe account shows a different business name ("Naturebylily")
    on invoices — confusing/trust-breaking for her center.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Trial-end email sequence (7/3/1 days) + in-app banner.
  - "Upgrade and continue" flow that preserves in-progress form.
  - Correct business identity on Stripe invoices.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 5 — ONGOING OPERATIONS (MONTHLY LOOP)  [EMOTION: 5 😐 strained]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS (weekly/monthly rhythm):
  - Adds new students; manages staff turnover.
  - Reviews reports; chases therapists about pending notes.
  - Tracks attendance OUTSIDE the app (paper/spreadsheet) and
    invoices parents manually.
  - Handles every "I forgot my password" personally.
TOUCHPOINTS: Students · Users · Reports · (paper!) · WhatsApp · phone
THOUGHTS: "Why am I still doing attendance and invoicing by hand?
That was half the reason I bought this."
PAIN POINTS:
  - No attendance module in UI (DB models exist, unused).
  - No parent invoicing.
  - Password resets = her job, several times a week.
  - No notifications when notes go overdue — she finds out late.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Attendance UI + per-session parent invoicing.
  - Self-serve password reset (email link) for staff AND parents.
  - Admin digest email: pending notes >48h, incomplete sessions.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 6 — RETENTION / CHURN POINT            [EMOTION: 6 🙂]
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIONS:
  - Decides whether to renew monthly or switch to annual.
  - Considers recommending the platform to 2 other center owners.
TOUCHPOINTS: Billing page · Stripe Portal · word of mouth
THOUGHTS: "If attendance + parent reports existed, I'd never
leave. Without them, I'm paying for 60% of my workflow."
PAIN POINTS:
  - Renewal decision is silent — no value recap of what the
    platform did for her this month.
OPPORTUNITIES:
  - Monthly "your center in numbers" email (sessions run, notes
    signed, parents engaged) → drives annual upgrade + referrals.
